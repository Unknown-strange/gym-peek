About this gym

Pulse Fitness Hub is a premier wellness and strength training facility situated in the heart of East Legon. Featuring state-of-the-art cardio machines, Olympic weightlifting platforms, air-conditioned studio spaces, and clean private shower facilities. Designed for both casual fitness enthusiasts and serious powerlifters looking for high-end equipment with zero fluff.
