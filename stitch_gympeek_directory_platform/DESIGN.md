## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a forward-looking, high-vitality lifestyle directory centered on physical spaces, transparency, and effortless discovery across urban fitness hubs. The personality balances precision architecture with athletic momentum: confident, transparent, invigorating, and meticulously curated.

The visual style blends **Liquid Glassmorphism** with clean modern surfaces. Interfaces feature crystalline frosted containers, fluid specular edges, luminous depth transitions, and deep athletic blues balanced against high-key architectural whites. Ambient cyan and energetic emerald radiance spill softly under focal action points, evoking modern wellness clubs, pristine iron gyms, and cutting-edge performance spaces. 

Interactions feel featherlight yet tactile: cards hover with soft refraction and elastic lifts, while high-intent navigational actions leverage rich kinetic gradients. The experience prioritizes immediate sensory reassurance through spatial previewing, transparent fee breakdowns, and definitive location routing.

## Colors

The palette establishes an energetic, premium visual balance engineered for high scanability under both direct sunlight and low-light mobile navigation.

- **Primary Blue (`#2563EB`)**: The central engine of the interface. Powers active states, focal navigation, filter highlights, brand badges, and dynamic image overlays.
- **Deep Navy (`#0B1220`)**: The core anchor. Provides deep contrast for primary typography, dark navigation bars, footer planes, and high-impact structural backdrops.
- **Accent Green (`#22C55E`)**: A high-utility signal color reserved strictly for action completion, "Get Directions" routing CTAs, real-time "Open Now" operating states, and active facility amenities.
- **Surface Canvas (`#F1F5F9`)**: A cool architectural off-white foundation that eliminates eye strain and allows white translucent glass surfaces to detach with depth.
- **Cards & Sheets (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure high-grade white providing crystalline base clarity under glassmorphic filters and translucent borders.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#CBD5E1`)**: Subtle structural dividers, calibrated at 40%–70% opacity to mimic the edges of cut glass.
- **Muted & Meta Text (`#64748B`)**: Neutral slate dedicated to operating hours, secondary metadata, equipment specs, and pricing terms.

## Typography

The typography system relies exclusively on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its sculpted geometric forms, contemporary humanist warmth, and athletic clarity.

- **Headlines**: Extra-bold and bold weights deliver punchy editorial impact. Tight negative letter-spacing creates optical tension that gives facility names and hero banners high energy and presence.
- **Body & Editorial**: Regular and medium weights provide effortless legibility for pricing tables, amenity rundowns, and membership breakdowns.
- **Labels & Badges**: Medium-to-bold compact scales with subtle positive tracking enhance visibility on frosted pill indicators, open/closed tags, and map overlays.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt fluid-responsive structure designed to highlight visual inventory:

- **Desktop (12 Columns, max-width 1280px)**: 32px gutters with 64px outer margins. Supports split search layouts (persistent responsive map alongside fluid card grids) or expansive 3-column discovery grids.
- **Tablet (8 Columns, 768px – 1024px)**: 24px gutters with 32px outer canvas margins. Adapts cards into 2-column arrays with quick-filter horizontal carousels.
- **Mobile (4 Columns, under 768px)**: 16px gutters with 16px outer edge margins. Converts list cards to full-width dynamic stacks with sticky bottom glass action docks.

Content rhythm prioritizes spatial breathing room (`space-lg` to `space-xl` between modular sections) contrasted with compact, high-density groupings (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) for metadata, ratings, and amenity tags.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through **Liquid Glassmorphism** layered over deep ambient illumination:

1. **Flat / Base Layer**: `#F1F5F9` canvas textured with subtle radial meshes of soft blue (`rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.06)`) and emerald (`rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.04)`).
2. **Surface Glass Layer (Cards, Modules)**: Solid `#FFFFFF` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with a 16px–24px backdrop blur, framed by a 1px border of `rgba(203, 213, 225, 0.6)`. Drops a diffused ambient shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(11, 18, 32, 0.06), 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(11, 18, 32, 0.04)`.
3. **Floating Glass Layer (Floating Navigation, Quick Filters, Overlays)**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` coupled with a 20px–30px backdrop blur, a hairline highlight border `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8), rgba(203, 213, 225, 0.3))`, and a colored kinetic underglow: `0 12px 36px -6px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.12)`.
4. **Interactive Raised State (Card Hover, Dropdown Focus)**: Translation of -3px vertically, with border opacity escalating to `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.35)` and shadow deepening to `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(11, 18, 32, 0.12), 0 0 16px 2px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy establishes a deliberate contrast between structured containers and organic interactive controls:

- **Large Structural Surfaces & Cards**: Fixed at `20px` border-radius (`rounded-[20px]`). This creates an architectural, contemporary look that frames gym photography and content blocks without feeling toy-like.
- **Floating Controls, Buttons & Chips**: Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full` / `9999px`). These soft, pebble-smooth contours invite touch and signify actionable kinetic touchpoints.
- **Micro-Indicators & Badges**: Fully pill-shaped with tailored horizontal padding to anchor numeric ratings, status indicators, and category tags.
- **Image Containers**: Matched to parent card contours (`20px` outer, `16px` nested) with clean specular clipping edges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Standard)**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), solid `#2563EB` fill, `#FFFFFF` bold typography, subtle specular inner glow (`inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`). Hover: dynamic shift to `#1D4ED8` with a soft blue halo shadow.
- **Directions Button (Dedicated)**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), solid `#22C55E` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, paired with a right arrow or navigation compass icon. Features a vibrant emerald underglow shadow (`0 8px 20px -4px rgba(34, 197, 94, 0.4)`).
- **Glass Secondary**: Semi-translucent white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`), 1px border (`#CBD5E1`), `#0B1220` text. Hover adds a crisp frosted highlight and subtle scale lift.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **State Neutral**: `rounded-full`, white or semi-transparent glass, 1px `#CBD5E1` border, `#64748B` label.
- **State Active**: `rounded-full`, solid `#0B1220` or `#2563EB` fill, crisp white typography, zero border, faint glow.
- **Status Indicator Chip**: Frosted miniature pill (`rounded-full`), backdrop blur, green dot with animated ping animation alongside `Open Now` in `#22C55E` bold label text.

### Directory Gym Cards
- **Structure**: High-grade white canvas, 20px rounded corners, 1px border of `rgba(203, 213, 225, 0.7)`.
- **Top Media Area**: Shimmer gradient placeholder box (ranging from deep navy `#0B1220` to `#1E3A8A` and primary `#2563EB` with an animated diagonal glass gloss line) adorned with a centered translucent frosted camera icon badge.
- **Card Body**: Bold `#0B1220` headline, location pin with neighborhood descriptor, visual price callout (e.g., `GHS 150 / mo` emphasized in `#0B1220` with bold numerical sizing), and an integrated bottom split: "View Gym" text trigger alongside the high-intent pill-shaped "Get directions" button.

### Form Inputs & Search Docks
- **Global Search Bar**: Floating liquid glass dock, `rounded-full`, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` surface, 1px `#CBD5E1` boundary, 24px blur. Left-anchored location pin / search icon, direct input, and right-anchored floating primary blue search pill.
- **Standard Field**: 14px rounded-xl input, crisp `#FFFFFF` fill, 1.5px `#CBD5E1` resting border, shifting to `#2563EB` border with a 4px soft blue ambient focus aura (`rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes**: 6px rounded squares, 1.5px `#CBD5E1` stroke. Checked state transitions instantly to `#2563EB` background with a crisp white check vector mark.
- **Radio Buttons**: Concentric circles, activating with a glowing `#2563EB` inner pip surrounded by a frosted clear gap.

### Map Floating Glass Tiles
- Pinned bottom-center or overlay glass cards showing mini gym previews: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`, 20px rounded corners, micro thumbnail, headline, pricing, and rapid directions icon button.
